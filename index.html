Thought Book

Your private space for sensitive thoughts

Thought Book is a password protected, offline first notes app for Windows. Your notes are encrypted and stored locally on your computer, giving you a private place to write down thoughts, ideas, and information without relying on an online service.

Why Thought Book?

  • Offline and Private: Your notes remain on your computer and are not dependent on an online notes service.
  • Stronger Encryption: Your notes are protected using modern cryptographic technology rather than a basic custom encryption method.
  • Password Protection: Protect your private notes with a password and use a password hint to help you remember it.
  • Cleaner Experience: The interface has been redesigned to be simpler, cleaner, and more professional.
  • Auto Lock: Automatically protect your notes when you leave the application unattended.
  • Auto Save: Your work can be saved automatically while you use the app.
  • Improved Performance: Version 2.0.1 brings faster startup, faster interface loading, and improved overall stability.
